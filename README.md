Summary of Insights
🔍 Ride demand and revenue peak during 7–10 AM and 5–8 PM, indicating commuter behavior.

📍 Top-performing zones include Whitefield, Koramangala, and Electronic City—vital for targeted operations.

💳 UPI is the most-used payment method; Cash still prevalent during evenings.

❌ Nearly 50% cancellations by both drivers and customers point to reliability and allocation issues.

✅ Completion after quotes is highest during peak hours, showing strong user intent when conditions are favorable.

Key Takeaways
Ensure dynamic driver deployment based on time and zone patterns.

Promote digital payments through UPI-focused incentives.

Reduce cancellations with better driver incentives and ETA accuracy.

Focus marketing efforts on low-performing zones and non-peak hours to balance demand.
